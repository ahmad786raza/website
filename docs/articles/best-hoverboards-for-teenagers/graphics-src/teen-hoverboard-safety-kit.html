Wear it every ride

The teen hoverboard safety kit

Five items, under the price of most boards, that cover the injuries emergency departments see most.

Helmet

CPSC-certified, snug, worn level

Wrist guards

The most common hoverboard injury is a wrist fracture

Knee and elbow pads

Sized for the rider, not borrowed

Lights or reflectives

For dusk rides; most boards only light the front

Closed, flat shoes

No sandals, no heels, laces tucked

Gear advice, not medical advice: supervise new riders and start on soft, flat groundhoverboardsguide.com
