Before you buy

The parent’s five-point check

1

UL 2272 mark

On the box, the board and the listing. It certifies the whole electrical system, not just the battery.

2

Weight limit with headroom

Pick a rating well above today’s weight; teenagers grow into a board within a year.

3

Speed and a beginner mode

7–10 mph is plenty. An app speed cap or beginner mode is worth more than a higher top speed.

4

Battery habits

Original charger only, never charge overnight, charge on a hard floor and unplug when the light turns green.

5

Warranty and support

Look for 12 months and a brand with US support. Keep the box for the first month.

Specs on listings are manufacturer claims; check the mark on the box before the first ridehoverboardsguide.com
