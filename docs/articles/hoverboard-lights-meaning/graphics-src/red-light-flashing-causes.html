Red light flashing

Six things a flashing red light usually means

1

Battery too low

The commonest cause. Charge fully before anything else; a board stored flat for months may need an hour before the light changes.

2

Not level at start-up

Switched on while tilted or on grass. Power off, set it on a hard, flat floor, power on again.

3

Gyro out of sync

The two halves disagree about “level”. A recalibration (hold power 5 to 10 seconds on flat ground) resets them.

4

Loose connector

A cable to a motor, sensor or the battery has worked loose, often after a drop. Flash count points to which side.

5

Overheating

Hot shell after a long climb or a hot car. Power off and let it cool for 30 to 60 minutes.

6

Count the flashes

Film the light in slow motion and count. The code table is in your manual; it differs between brands.

Work through them in this order; most boards are fixed by the first threehoverboardsguide.com
