The light on the charger brick

Charger light meanings

Nearly every 42V hoverboard charger uses the same two colours. Read it with the board switched off.

Red while plugged in

Charging. Normal. Expect 2 to 4 hours from empty; unplug when it turns green.

Normal

Green after a few hours

Full. Unplug. Leaving it on the charger overnight is the habit to break.

Unplug

Green the moment you plug in

No current is flowing. Check the port pins and cable, then try a known-good charger. If both fail, the battery or its protection board is the problem.

Check port and charger

Alternating red and green

The charger has detected a fault: damaged cable, wrong charger, overheated pack or a failing battery. Stop charging.

Stop charging
Use the charger that came with the board, or a UL-listed 42V 2A replacement with the same plughoverboardsguide.com
